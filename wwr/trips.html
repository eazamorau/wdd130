Trips

Whether you are looking for a relaxing family adventure or an exciting challenge through powerful rapids, ZAGA Rafting has a trip for you. Explore our rafting experiences and choose the adventure that best matches your skill level and sense of adventure.

River Explorer — Beginner

River Explorer is a relaxing and family-friendly rafting experience designed for beginners. Enjoy beautiful mountain scenery, gentle currents, and small rapids while learning the basics of rafting with an experienced guide. It is a great choice for families and first-time rafters who want adventure without the extreme intensity.

Rapid Rush — Intermediate

Rapid Rush offers the perfect balance between excitement and control. Rafters will navigate stronger currents, faster water, and moderate rapids while working together as a team. This trip is ideal for adventure seekers who are ready for more action but still want a fun and manageable rafting experience.

Extreme Canyon — Advanced

Extreme Canyon is built for experienced rafters looking for a serious challenge. Powerful rapids, steep drops, rushing water, and narrow rocky passages make this an intense and unforgettable adventure. Strong teamwork, confidence, and previous rafting experience are recommended.
